PRIVACY POLICY - ClipMaster (Free Version)

ClipMaster values your privacy. Here's how we handle your data:

1. Data Collection: ClipMaster stores your clipboard history locally on your device. We do not collect, upload, or transmit any data to external servers.

2. Screenshots & Clipboard: These are saved only locally and never shared unless you export/share them manually.

3. No Cloud/Internet Use: ClipMaster is an offline-first application. No cloud syncing or online tracking is used.

4. Security: We do not access or store user credentials, personal files, or sensitive data.

5. Automatic Updates: ClipMaster may check for updates automatically, but no personal data is transmitted during this process.

6. GDPR/CCPA: As ClipMaster does not process personal data, these regulations do not directly apply. No user profiling is performed.

7. Local Storage Only: All data including clipboard history, screenshots, and settings are stored exclusively on your device.

8. No User Accounts: ClipMaster does not require user registration or login. No personal information is collected.

For cloud features and cross-device sync, please consider ClipMaster Pro.
